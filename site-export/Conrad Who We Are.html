ASX Listed and Singapore Headquartered with an International Team

Conrad Asia Energy (ASX:CRD) is building a significant independent gas business in Southeast Asia — identifying, developing and producing natural gas from undervalued offshore assets to supply the world's fastest-growing energy market. Conrad delivers affordable, lower-carbon energy to the region's expanding economies, creating long-term value for shareholders, partners and communities.

Headquartered in Singapore and listed on the ASX since October 2022, Conrad operates three Production Sharing Contracts in Indonesia. The Mako Gas Project is fully funded and on track to deliver first gas by end-2027. Conrad also holds 100% interest in two Aceh PSCs.

Milestones

Our History

2007

Duyung PSC awarded

The Duyung Production Sharing Contract is awarded to West Natuna Exploration Limited (WNEL) in the West Natuna Sea, approximately 400 km northeast of Singapore. The PSC covers 4,641 sq km and runs until January 2037, forming the foundation for the Mako Gas Field.

2010

Company founded — Conrad Petroleum Ltd.

Miltos Xynogalas founds the company in Singapore (then Conrad Petroleum Ltd., later renamed Conrad Asia Energy) with a focus on identifying undervalued, overlooked, and technically misunderstood gas assets in offshore Indonesia.

Apr 2017

Empyrean Energy farms in (10% WI)

Empyrean Energy signs a sale and purchase agreement to acquire a 10% interest in West Natuna Exploration Ltd (WNEL), providing capital ahead of the maiden discovery well and marking Conrad's first external partnership.

Jun 2017

Mako South-1 discovery well drilled

The Mako South-1 well is drilled to 520 m total depth, confirming 23 ft of net gas pay with 98% methane purity. It demonstrates commercial viability of the Mako field — the largest undeveloped gas discovery in the West Natuna Basin.

Feb 2018

First Plan of Development approved

Indonesia approves the original Plan of Development (POD) for the Mako Gas Field, formally converting the Duyung PSC from an exploration to an exploitation licence and extending PSC tenure. Signals early government backing for commercialisation.

2019

Tambak-2 appraisal well + Coro Energy farm-in

The Tambak-2 appraisal well successfully delineates the southern extent of the Mako field, converting significant 3C resources to 2C. In the same period, Coro Energy acquires 15% PI and Empyrean's interest is restructured to 8.5% PI directly in the PSC.

Sep 2022

Peter Botten appointed Non-Executive Chairman

Former Oil Search MD and AGL Chairman Peter Botten, AC CBE, is appointed Non-Executive Chairman ahead of the ASX IPO — bringing major ASX investor credibility and deep Asia-Pacific upstream experience to Conrad's board.

27 Oct 2022

ASX listing & A$45M IPO

Conrad Asia Energy (ASX: CRD) lists on the Australian Securities Exchange, raising A$45 million via 30.8 million CDIs at A$1.46 each. Proceeds earmarked for Mako FEED studies, Aceh joint study areas, and seismic acquisition.

Nov 2022

Revised Mako POD approved — resources up 458%

The Indonesian Ministry of Energy approves a revised Plan of Development for the Mako Gas Field following the 2019 drilling campaigns. Contingent 2C resources increase by 458% to 413 Bcf gross, materially de-risking the project and setting a new capex framework.

Nov 2022

Aceh exploration blocks (ONWA & OSWA) awarded

Indonesia awards Conrad the Offshore North West Aceh (ONWA/Meulaboh) and Offshore South West Aceh (OSWA/Singkil) PSCs — 100% operated, ~20,000 sq km combined, containing three existing shallow-water gas discoveries and multi-Tcf deep-water potential.

Jan 2023

Aceh PSCs formally signed — 30-year tenure

Conrad formally signs the ONWA and OSWA Production Sharing Contracts with the Indonesian government, each carrying a 30-year tenure and a US$15M committed work programme. Transforms Conrad from a single-asset company to a multi-project gas developer.

Nov 2023

FEED studies concluded + Aceh resources >11 Tcf net

Front-End Engineering and Design (FEED) studies for the Mako development are completed, locking in Phase capex at ~US$325M. Conrad also announces first Aceh Prospective Resources estimate exceeding 11 Tcf net — highlighting the deep-water upside of the Aceh blocks.

Feb 2024

A$16M capital raise (placement + SPP)

Conrad completes a A$13M institutional placement at A$0.95 per CDI, followed by a Share Purchase Plan raising ~A$3M. Proceeds support Aceh exploration studies, 3D seismic planning, and advancement of the Mako gas sales agreements ahead of FID.

Jun 2024

Binding PGN gas sales agreement signed

Conrad signs a binding GSA with PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (PGN), Indonesia's largest gas company, committing the domestic gas tranche of Mako's production — a key milestone on the path to FID and a signal of strong Indonesian government support.

Sep 2024

Binding Sembcorp GSA signed — all resources committed

A binding GSA is signed with Singapore's Sembcorp Gas for the export tranche of Mako gas, covering up to 76.9 million standard cubic feet per day through 2037. Combined with the PGN deal, 100% of Mako's contingent gas resources are now committed to buyers, clearing the path to FID.

Mar 2025

MEMR directive: all Mako gas redirected to domestic market

Indonesia's Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (MEMR) directs that all Mako gas be sold domestically in Batam, driven by surging Indonesian domestic demand. The PGN and Sembcorp GSAs are revoked; Conrad works with PLN and SKK Migas on a replacement GSA.

Jul 2025

Landmark GSA signed with PLN EPI — oil-linked pricing

Conrad signs a binding GSA with PLN EPI (a subsidiary of Indonesia's state-owned power utility PLN Persero, BBB-rated) for 100% of Mako's gas at oil-linked ICP pricing through 2037 — up to 111 Bbtud. PLN EPI also commits to building connecting pipeline infrastructure at no cost to Conrad.

Nov 2025

Nations Natuna Barat farm-down deal — full development carry

Conrad announces a transformative deal with PT Nations Natuna Barat (NNB/Arsari Group): NNB acquires 75% PI in the Duyung PSC and fully funds 100% of Mako Phase 1 development costs (~US$320M) including carrying WNEL's 25% share, eliminating Conrad's equity funding requirements for the project.

3 Mar 2026

Final Investment Decision (FID) approved — US$320M development

FID is approved for the Mako Gas Project, transitioning Conrad from speculative explorer to fully contracted gas developer. Phase 1 covers six subsea wells tied back to a leased 172-MMscfd MOPU. Over 80% of US$320M capex contracted by end of Q1 2026. First gas targeted Q4 2027.

Jun 2026

Leadership restructure + drilling rig contracted

Peter Botten becomes Executive Chairman; Miltos Xynogalas transitions to an Indonesian-focused executive director role from September 2026; a CEO search is launched. Separately, WNEL executes a binding rig contract with the PDSI-ADES consortium (Admarine 502 jackup) for six development wells, commencing Q2 2027.
