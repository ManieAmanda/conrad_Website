Reserves & Resources

Conrad's portfolio combines independently assessed contingent resources at the Mako Gas Field with substantial prospective resources across the Aceh PSCs — a balance of near-term, fully funded development volumes and significant exploration upside.

Resources Summary
Asset Category Volume Basis
Mako Gas Field (Duyung PSC) 2C Contingent ~413 Bcf gross GaffneyCline CPR(1)
Aceh PSCs (ONWA & OSWA) Prospective >11 Tcf net ASX release 13 Nov 2023(2)

(1) Competent Person's Report (CPR) for Duyung by Gaffney, Cline & Associates (GaffneyCline), 26 August 2022. Resources attributable to Conrad are based on Conrad's 76.5% participating interest in the Duyung PSC.

(2) ASX Release — 13 November 2023: "Aceh: Prospective Resources in Excess of 11 Tcf (Net)".

About These Estimates

The Mako 2C contingent resource estimate reflects the revised Plan of Development worked up with SKK Migas following the successful 2017 discovery and 2019 appraisal campaigns. Mako gas is high quality — 98% methane with no mercury or heavy metals — requiring no LNG refrigeration or regasification.

The Aceh prospective resources reflect the maiden independent assessment of the ONWA and OSWA blocks, combining three existing shallow-water gas discoveries with multi-Tcf deep-water exploration potential. Prospective resources are estimated volumes associated with undiscovered accumulations and carry exploration risk.
