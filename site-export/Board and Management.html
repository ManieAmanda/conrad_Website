Leadership

Our Board

An experienced team with a proven track record of value creation in Asian energy markets.

Peter Botten

Executive Chairman

  • Extensive worldwide experience in the oil and gas industry, having held various senior technical, managerial and board positions in a number of listed and government-owned bodies.
  • Previously Chairman of AGL, Australia's largest energy retailer, and former Managing Director of Oil Search Limited, overseeing its development into a major ASX-listed company from 1994 until 2020.
  • Current directorships include Karoon Energy Limited (ASX: KAR) and Aurelia Minerals Limited (ASX: AMI); Council Member of the Australia PNG Business Council; and Chairman of the Oil Search Foundation, the Hela Provincial Health Authority and the National Football Stadium Trust in Papua New Guinea.

Miltos Xynogalas

Founder, Managing Director & CEO

  • Geoscientist with over 30 years of upstream experience, at least half of it gained in Indonesia.
  • Prior to founding Conrad, worked for Shell International, Premier Oil and Transworld Oil in roles ranging from technical and operational to supervisory and managerial.
  • The main focus of his career has been exploration and development projects and, more recently, business development, particularly in Indonesia.
  • Has been involved in major discoveries in South East and West Africa.

David Johnson

Executive Director & COO

  • Geoscientist and General Manager with 41 years of E&P experience across the full spectrum of upstream activities: exploration, development, production, business development and decommissioning.
  • Has worked in Australia, the Asia-Pacific and the Middle East with BP, Shell, Woodside, Mubadala Petroleum and Ophir Energy/Medco Energi, with his career focused on project appraisal, development and production.

Paul Bernard

Non-Executive Director

  • Retired Goldman Sachs partner and private investor; during his 19-year career at the firm he was a top-rated Asian energy & chemicals analyst as well as co-Director of Asia Pacific Investment Research.
  • CFA charterholder and member of the firm's Asia Management Committee, and its first Chairman of Diversity for Asia.
  • Since retiring from Goldman Sachs, has been an early-stage investor in and advisor to a number of companies.
  • Current directorships include Biotech Acquisition Co, Sandbox International Holdings Ltd, Sandbox Edutainment Holdings Ltd, Castle European and TTS Advisors Pte Ltd.

Jeremy Brest

Non-Executive Director

  • Founder and Managing Director of Framework Capital Solutions, a Singapore boutique corporate finance advisory firm focused on structured private transactions.
  • Over more than 15 years has served as sole financial advisor on debt restructurings, private credit transactions and M&A transactions around the world.
  • Director of Pantheon Resources Plc, an AIM-listed company with 100% working interests in several oil projects on the Alaskan North Slope.
  • Prior to founding Framework, worked at Goldman Sachs in New York, Hong Kong and Tokyo, and led the Indonesian credit structuring team for Credit Suisse in the wake of the Asian financial crisis.

Mario Traviati

Non-Executive Director

  • Nearly four decades of experience working, analysing and investing in energy projects around the world.
  • Currently an Advisor to the Board — Corporate Development at Pantheon Resources (London AIM-listed company).
  • Previously Founding Partner and Vice President of Business Development for Great Bear Petroleum, which operates oil and gas properties on the North Slope of Alaska.
  • First Vice President — Head of Energy Research Asia-Pacific at Merrill Lynch Inc., supervising research across 10 Asia-Pacific countries covering oil and gas, utilities, refining and marketing, and petrochemicals.
  • Prior to Merrill Lynch, served as Director of Energy Research at HSBC Securities and as a Senior Energy Analyst with ANZ Securities; began his oil and gas career in exploration with Woodside Petroleum.
Management Team

Our Management

Day-to-day execution led by a focused, experienced team across Singapore and Indonesia.

Harsh Murarka

Financial Advisor

Harsh is an experienced energy corporate finance and investment banking professional with 16 years' experience across capital raising, M&A and corporate finance in Asia. Harsh started his career with the M&A team at Lazard and has subsequently held senior investment banking roles at Pareto Securities and Natixis covering energy investment banking in Asia. He has also previously held CFO and Head of Corporate Finance roles with HBA Future Energy and GAINN Group. Harsh brings diversified transaction experience having closed 50+ deals (~$6bn deal value).

Sally Ting

General Counsel

Sally brings 25+ years experience in legal, advisory and business development roles in APAC, including 10+ years with law firms such as King & Wood Mallesons and Milbank LLP as well as 15+ years in-house experience in SE Asian focused oil & gas companies such as Salamander Energy and KrisEnergy.

Patricia Lee

Head of Finance

Patricia brings 25+ years experience in finance and FP&A in APAC, with 15+ years focused experience within oil & gas in SE Asia. Patricia was previously Manager, Finance and HR at Mandala Energy Ltd, and Premier Oil Fast East Limited.

Danial Murtadho

Acting GM, WNEL

Danial brings 25+ years experience in Indonesian oil & gas, having led various gas commercialisations through his career. Danial has previously held senior roles at ConocoPhillips and Mubadala, having delivered three gas sales agreements worth over US$3bn, and has been closely involved in the delivery of a US$500m offshore gas project from FID through production to abandonment.
