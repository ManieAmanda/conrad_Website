What We Do

Aceh PSCs

Conrad holds two 100%-operated exploration PSCs offshore Aceh in northern Sumatra — Offshore North West Aceh (ONWA) and Offshore South West Aceh (OSWA). Together they cover roughly 20,000 km², contain three existing shallow-water gas discoveries, and host prospective resources in excess of 11 Tcf net.

Overview

Conrad holds 100% operated interest in the Offshore North West Aceh (ONWA) PSC and the Offshore South West Aceh (OSWA) PSC. The PSCs, spread across a combined acreage of ~20,000 square kilometres, hold three existing shallow-water gas discoveries with a significant 2C contingent resource base of 216 Bcf (162 Bcf net attributable to Conrad after government take) located close to shore, with various commercialisation options being evaluated.

The PSCs also hold additional near-field shallow-water appraisal and exploration potential (unrisked P50 546 Bcf, 394 Bcf net attributable). In addition, the PSCs represent over 15 Tcf (unrisked P50, 11 Tcf net attributable) of deep-water prospects across 38 leads, of which four are larger than 1 Tcf each.
