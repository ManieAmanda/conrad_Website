Duyung PSC & Mako Gas Field

The Duyung PSC contains the Mako Gas Field, the largest undeveloped gas field in the South Natuna Sea, containing 2P reserves of 330 Bcf (Gaffney Cline & Associates, gross 100% basis) in one continuous "single tank" of high-quality gas (>98% methane, no mercury, no heavy metals).

Located approximately 100 km to the north of Matak Island and about 400 km northeast of Singapore, the Mako Gas Field was discovered and appraised by Conrad in 2017 and 2019, respectively, and the plan of development (POD) was approved in 2022. 100% of the gas is contracted to be sold to PLN EPI at attractive oil-linked gas prices.

Following a successful farm-down and FID in 1Q 2026, the $320 million project is fully-funded with a substantial contingency and on-track to deliver first gas by end-2027. The development plan consists of 6 subsea wells tied into a MOPU with compression and processing which connect into the existing West Natuna Transportation System (WNTS) to supply gas to Batam.

This is Conrad's anchor asset in which Conrad retains 22.875% economic interest and continues to be the operator. Once operational, Mako is expected to provide Conrad a stable cash flow base.
