Project Overview

Conrad Asia Energy, through its wholly owned subsidiaries, holds operated interests in offshore Indonesia Production Sharing Contracts (PSCs). The Duyung PSC — which contains the Mako Gas Field — is the cornerstone of the portfolio, complemented by the Offshore North West Aceh (ONWA) and Offshore South West Aceh (OSWA) exploration PSCs. Together they combine a fully funded, near-term development with substantial multi-Tcf exploration upside.
