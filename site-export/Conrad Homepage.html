Conrad Asia Energy Ltd is a Southeast Asian upstream oil & gas company led by a strong senior management team with proven technical, operational and financial expertise. Conrad is headquartered in Singapore and listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX:CRD) since 2022.

Conrad has a strong operational presence in Indonesia where it operates 3 production sharing contracts (PSCs). Conrad's Mako Gas Project in the Duyung PSC is fully funded and on-track to deliver first gas by end-2027. Conrad also has 100% interest in 2 PSCs in Aceh with 4 existing gas discoveries. With the Mako project underway, Conrad is commencing commercialization activities on the Aceh PSCs as well as actively evaluating other inorganic growth opportunities more broadly in Southeast Asia.
